# Changelog

## 1.1

- The settings are described rows (winxi 65): every setting is its name, a sentence under the name saying what it does, and its control at the right, and the (?) marks and their tooltips are gone. An event's row is its switch, with its sound and Test button in a row under it, and its threshold or the items named under that: those rows are greyed while the event is off, not hidden. Volume is a dropdown; Overlap Sounds is marked experimental. On the Triggers tab, Mob Type, Action and Create Trigger (its button reading Create, the row marked experimental) are rows under New Trigger; the triggers are a table with their switch, name, sound, Test and Remove; and the mobs of the chosen kind in your area, which the (?) used to say, are the line at the foot of the tab. The Toast tab is one section: Enable Toast, then Duration, Fade In And Out, Grow From (a dropdown now, not three buttons) and the hiding rules under it, greyed while it is off.

## 1.0 — first release

- A sound when something happens: a tell, an invite, a trade, being examined, a mention, an item you are after dropping, a skill-up, low HP or MP, TP ready, being knocked out.
- A spoken voice for each, a volume for all of them, and sounds that can overlap.
- An optional indicator on the screen.
- Experimental: triggers of your own, on a monster's skill or spell.
