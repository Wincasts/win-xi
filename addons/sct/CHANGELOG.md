# Changelog

## 0.8 — first release

- Scrolling text for your own statuses: `+ [icon] Name` as you gain one, `- [icon] Name` as you lose one, down a box and fading out. Buffs blue, enfeebles purple, anything lost grey, each colour your own.
- Items received into your Inventory, with their pictures and quantities, and key items gained. Items moved in from your other bags, and what you already had at load or on zoning, are not shown.
- The icons are winxi's: your Custom Icons set where it has one for the status, the game's own otherwise.
- Text Only or Full Background, the box's size, speed, spacing and alignment. `/sct test` and `/sct testdrop` send samples down the box.
