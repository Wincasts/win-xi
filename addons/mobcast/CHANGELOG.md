# Changelog

## 0.4 — first release

- mobcast shows what the enemies near you are casting, readying and using, in a small window, a line
  for each, worded as the chat log says it and coloured by kind. Each line follows the action through:
  begun, cast or used, or interrupted.
- The settings are described rows: each has its name, a sentence saying what it does, and its control.
  The Window section comes first (Lock Window, Position, Grow From), then what is shown.
- **Newest Line** (Bottom or Top) in the Box settings.
- An enemy that is paralyzed no longer shows as casting "Spell 0".
